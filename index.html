student life balance

Student life can be busy and stressful. Many students study for long hours and forget to rest. A good balance helps students stay healthy and focused.

This web site is about study habits, daily routines, hobbies, and useful tools. Good planning helps students use time well. Clear goals help students stay on track.

This project also shows html features such as links, images, tables, and lists. It uses css for style and design. Simple habits and smart choices can make student life better.
