daily routine

A daily routine helps students use time in a better way.
Without a routine, it is easy to waste time or forget important work. A simple plan can make the day less stressful.

Good routines include study time, meal time, short breaks, and enough sleep. Planning ahead helps students stay organized. Regular habits also improve focus and reduce last-minute pressure.

The table below shows one simple example of a student routine. It is not a strict rule, but it gives a clear idea of how a day can be balanced.

time activity reason
8:00 am get ready for the day start calmly
10:00 am study session finish important work
1:00 pm lunch break rest and eat
3:00 pm assignment work keep progress steady
7:00 pm free time relax and recover
